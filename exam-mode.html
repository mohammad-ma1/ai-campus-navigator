Exam Prep Mode

What kind of help do you need today?

Pick a mode. Get prompts that actually work for studying.

Explain a topic

Make a hard concept click in 3 minutes.

1
Explain [topic] to a 1st-year engineering student. Use a simple analogy, then a 4-line summary.
2
Re-explain [topic] but this time go one level deeper. Add the math.
3
Where do students usually get [topic] wrong? List the 3 most common misconceptions.

Make flashcards

Turn your notes into active-recall cards.

1
Turn these notes into 20 flashcards. Question on one side, a short answer on the other. Notes: …
2
Make 10 flashcards on [topic] focusing only on definitions and formulas.
3
From these flashcards, which 5 are most likely to appear on an exam? Why?

Practice questions

Train on problems like the real exam.

1
Generate 10 practice questions on [topic] with progressive difficulty. Hide the solutions at the end.
2
Here is a past exam question. Make 3 similar questions with different numbers: …
3
Give me one hard problem on [topic]. Don't show the solution until I try.

Quiz me

Let the AI test you, one question at a time.

1
Quiz me on [chapter]. One question at a time. Tell me if I'm right and why.
2
Ask me 5 conceptual 'why' questions about [topic]. Rate my answers from 1 to 5.
3
Act as my professor in an oral exam on [topic]. Ask follow-up questions when my answer is weak.

Summarize my notes

Compress a semester into one page.

1
Summarize my notes into key points, definitions, and 5 likely exam questions. Notes: …
2
Create a one-page cheat sheet of formulas for [course], grouped by topic.
3
Compare my summary with these lecture notes. What important points did I miss?

Build a full study session

Combine modes: explain → flashcards → quiz → summarize.
