Top tips

I want help with coding

Debug faster, understand deeper, and stay sharp.

Recommended tools

ChatGPTClaudeGitHub CopilotCursor

Top tips

  • Paste the exact error message and the smallest piece of code that reproduces it.
  • Ask for an explanation of the fix — not just the fixed code.
  • Re-type the solution yourself so you actually learn it.

Starter prompts

Help me debug this code. Explain the bug step by step. Don't rewrite unless needed: …
I'm getting this error: '<paste>'. Explain what it means and 3 things to check first.
Review my function for edge cases and suggest 5 test inputs: …

I want to research safely

Find sources you can actually cite — not invented ones.

Recommended tools

PerplexityGeminiClaude

Top tips

  • Use AI search tools that show their sources, then open every link.
  • Never cite a paper you haven't seen with your own eyes.
  • Use AI to understand papers, not to replace reading them.

Starter prompts

Find 5 peer-reviewed sources on [topic] from the last 5 years. Give links and a one-line summary each.
Summarize this paper: problem, method, results, limitations. Paper: …
What are the main open debates in [field]? Who are the key researchers on each side?

I want to understand AI risks

Use AI without losing your privacy, ethics, or grades.

Recommended tools

Any tool — used carefully

Top tips

  • Check your course's AI policy before every assignment.
  • Never paste IDs, passwords, medical info, or unpublished research.
  • Treat confident answers as claims to verify, not facts.

Starter prompts

What are the most common ways an AI answer about [topic] could be wrong?
Here is my AI-assisted draft. Which parts would my professor consider my own work?
Remove all personal information from this text before I use it: …
