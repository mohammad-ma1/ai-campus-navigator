Prompt Battle

Weak Prompt vs Strong Prompt

Same question, very different answers. The prompt is the experiment — improve it and your AI gets smarter.

BATTLE #1

Math concept

WEAK

Explain calculus.

STRONG
Explain derivatives to a first-year engineering student using a real-life example (e.g. car speed). Then give me 3 practice questions with hidden solutions.

Why it wins: Names the level, the concept, the example, and the exact output you want.

BATTLE #2

Code debugging

WEAK

Fix my code.

STRONG
Help me debug this Python function. Walk through the bug step by step. Don't rewrite the whole thing — just fix the problem and explain why. ```python … ```

Why it wins: Says what to do, what NOT to do, and gives the actual code.

BATTLE #3

Summarizing

WEAK

Summarize this paper.

STRONG
Summarize this paper for a final-year CS student. Use 5 sections: problem, method (in plain words), results, limitations, and 2 questions I should ask in class. Paper: …

Why it wins: Defines the audience, structure, length, and the angle.

BATTLE #4

Study planning

WEAK

Help me prepare for my exam.

STRONG
Make a 7-day study plan for my Signals & Systems midterm. I have 3 hours per day. Mix new topics, review, and practice problems. Prioritize topics I'm weak on: convolution and Fourier series.

Why it wins: Includes time budget, topics, weaknesses, and the desired mix of activities.

BATTLE #5

Writing

WEAK

Improve this paragraph.

STRONG
Improve the clarity and grammar of this paragraph. Keep my voice and arguments. Show what you changed and why, in a short list. Don't add new content. Paragraph: …

Why it wins: Sets boundaries: what to keep, what to change, what NOT to add.

BATTLE #6

Brainstorming

WEAK

Give me project ideas.

STRONG
Suggest 10 final-year project ideas combining IoT and machine learning for a computer engineering student. For each: difficulty (1–5), key skills, main risk, one related paper.

Why it wins: Specifies count, domain, audience, and structured output.
