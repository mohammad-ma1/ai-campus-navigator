The essentials

LLM Survival Kit for Students

Print it. Pin it. Live by it. Five short lists that cover 90% of what you need.

5 best prompts

  • Explain [concept] to a [year] student with an analogy, then 3 practice questions.
  • Quiz me on [topic]. One question at a time. Tell me if I'm right and why.
  • Summarize this into key points, definitions, and 5 likely exam questions: …
  • Help me debug this code. Explain the bug step by step. Don't rewrite unless needed: …
  • Make a [N]-day study plan for my [course] exam, [hours]/day. Mix new + review + practice.

5 mistakes to avoid

  • Trusting confident-sounding answers without checking.
  • Asking vague prompts and expecting magic.
  • Pasting personal IDs, medical info, or unpublished research.
  • Submitting AI-written work as your own.
  • Skipping the part where YOU think.

5 privacy rules

  • Never paste IDs, passport numbers, or login info.
  • Anonymize names before pasting emails or messages.
  • Don't share other people's work without permission.
  • Crop or blur screenshots that show credentials.
  • When in doubt, ask: would I post this publicly?

5 ways to verify answers

  • Ask AI to cite its sources — and check the links.
  • Cross-check with at least one course-recommended source.
  • Ask the same question two different ways and compare.
  • For numbers and dates, demand a year and a source.
  • Re-explain the answer in your own words. If you can't, you don't know it.

5 ways to use AI without cheating

  • Use AI to understand, not to produce graded work.
  • Use it to plan and structure — write the words yourself.
  • Use it as a study partner: explain, quiz, brainstorm.
  • Disclose AI assistance when your course requires it.
  • Always check your course's AI policy. If unsure, ask your professor.

You've got the kit. Now go use it.

Curiosity + AI + your own thinking = an unfair advantage.
